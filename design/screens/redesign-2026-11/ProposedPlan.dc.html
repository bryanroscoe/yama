How it plays at Bracket 4

Place −1/−1 counters efficiently, draw when your creatures receive them, and drain the controller when opposing creatures receive them.

Yawgmoth, Blowfly Infestation, the tutors and fast mana turn the counter shell into a decisive engine. Hapatra, Nest of Scarabs and Yawgmoth with Grave Venerations or Zulaport Cutthroat can repeat with tokens, bounded by your library and life.

Pace is untested. Talk the plan and its combos through with the table.
