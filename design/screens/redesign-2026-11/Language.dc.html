Yama design language

Night table: a near-black table, so commander art and card pictures carry the color. Nine rules for every page.

1. Art first

Commander art_crop, from Scryfall Art: [artist], © Wizards Revival Trance
Every deck, plan and card shows its picture. Darken with a wash, never a filter. Credit the artist.

2. One primary, three more, then More

Edit list Check cards Share More
More floats over the page. It never opens inline or pushes things down.

3. Two densities

Laptop 403632 Phone 5244
Same controls, same order. Only the size changes.

4. Tabs, not folds

Cards 100 Stats Plan 3 left History
3-5 sticky tabs. What's under a tab is open. At most one fold per section.

5. Both cards on every change

InOut Demonic Tutorreplaces Stitch Together
The card going in, then the one it replaces. Tap either for the full card.

6. Nothing touches

A text box 16 Review changesReset
Gaps, never margins: 16 above any row of buttons, 32 between sections.

7. No micro copy

No Anyone with the link Friends you send it to can see and copy the list. It isn't listed anywhere.
Yes Anyone with the link?
Headings, labels and buttons do the work. Anything more goes behind one "?" per section.

8. State in one word

Waiting for your OK In progress Archived
Version 32, from commander-decks on 5 Nov One pill per thing. Version, date and source are a plain line, never a chip.

9. Phone mirrors laptop

The same tabs, sections and order. The side column drops below; More becomes a sheet.
