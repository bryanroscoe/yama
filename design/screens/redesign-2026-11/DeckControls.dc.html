Menus and controls

Every menu, select, sheet and switch on the deck page and its pages, as it is today and as it should be. House rules: a menu holds at most about 7 actions of one kind; destructive actions last and apart; 2 to 5 choices are a segmented control or buttons; long pickers search; items sit on one 44 px grid with 12 to 16 px padding. Line numbers are in AUDIT.md.

Third pass: density and page structure (Bryan, 8 Nov)

On a laptop: controls 36 px, the primary 40 px, row actions 32 px, secondaries with 10 px corners. Phones keep 44 px. Rows A to F supersede rows 1 to 5 below where they differ.

A. Button size on a laptop

Today

48 to 56 px pills everywhere, at phone size. A few in a row fill the hero and wrap.

What changed

The primary is a 40 px pill. Secondaries are 36 px with 10 px corners and a 16 px icon. Row actions (Show all, collapse, Skip) are 32 px. Segmented controls are 36 px tracks.
