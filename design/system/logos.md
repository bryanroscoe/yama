# Logos

Bryan picked these on 20 Oct 2026 (repo `design/logo/`); a starting point that can still be refined.

- `yama-wordmark.svg`: YAMA in `#f2efe8` for dark grounds. viewBox 280 × 110; the peaks rise 6 units above the cap line, so align it by its baseline. Single ink: on a canvas, inline it with `fill="currentColor"` and `aria-label="Yama"`, about 28 px tall in the rail and 64 px on sign-in.
- `yama-icon.svg`: the two peaks, near `#f2efe8`, far `#a9adb3`, full bleed on `#0b0c0e`. For app icons; the OS rounds the corners.
- `yama-logo-512.png`: the icon rendered at 512 px.

No Wizards marks, mana symbols or card art. Wherever the name appears in full, keep "Unofficial fan software, not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast."
