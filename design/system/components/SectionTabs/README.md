# SectionTabs

A sticky strip of 3-5 section names under a page's header, so a long page is a few sections you switch between instead of many disclosures you scroll through.

- Underline tabs, page-level navigation that must read above a page's filters and segmented controls: labels in the display face, bold, 24 px (20 px on phones), `dim`, the current one `text` with a 3 px underline; a count or short state in the body face at 13 px beside a label. 16 px of space above the strip, 32 px between tabs (24 px on phones), at least 52 px tall. (Bryan, 11 Nov 2026: at 15 px body text the strip was hard to find under the pill filters.)
- Either real subpages (a deck: Cards, Stats, Plan, History) or jump links to sections that all stay open (a plan: Changes, Questions, Brackets). Never both on one page.
- Sticks to the top when the header scrolls away; scrolls sideways on a phone.
- Content under a tab is open by default. A disclosure is only for a method or a long list nobody needs by default, at most one per section.
