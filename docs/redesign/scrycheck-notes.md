# ScryCheck: what to borrow (looked at 8 Nov 2026)

Bryan: "We should get some hints from ScryCheck, as it's trying to do some of the stuff that we're also doing, especially for decks."
Page looked at: https://scrycheck.com/deck/eimz6vprwuhq08ql (Bryan's Rootha / Prismari Artistry list, pasted, signed out).
Three tabs: Analysis, Deck list, Insights. Dark UI, card images everywhere.

## Worth borrowing
1. **Three stat tiles in the deck header**: Power 7.5 (±0.4, "Optimized"), Bracket 4, Win turn T7 ("Typical, T6-T9+").
   Yama version: Bracket (declared, and what the list measures), Value ($, dated source), Record (games, wins) or Lab strength. Big numbers in the display face, one short word under each.
2. **"Why this bracket" with markers**: Game Changers 7 (each named), 2-card combos 2, mass land denial none, extra turns none,
   then "0.5 above the Bracket 3 ceiling". Yama already has Game Changers per bracket and combos in research: show the same
   checklist beside the bracket switch, so changing bracket shows which markers drop out.
3. **Deck list controls**, all visible, none hidden in a menu: view (pictures / list / table) as three icon buttons;
   Group by (card type, etc.); Sort as a segmented control (Mana cost, Name, Price); Copy; total price at the top right.
4. **A stacked type bar** (Creatures, Sorceries, Instants, Artifacts, Enchantments, Lands, sized by count) that also jumps to each group.
5. **Group headers** with count and the group's price subtotal; groups collapse.
6. **Mana curve with a compare-to-bracket overlay**, average mana value, low/mid/high counts, and a plain one-line verdict.
7. **Color balance**: share of colored symbols vs share of sources, per color, with a one-line verdict.
8. **Themes**: each with its core card count, one sentence, and the first five cards ("View 13 more").
9. **Combos** as card pairs (A + B).
10. **Insights / recommendations**: a named gap ("11 token producers, 1 way to turn them into damage"), a tip, and
    "Add options" as a row of card pictures, "popular in similar decks". The same shape fits Yama plan proposals.
11. **History**: a snapshot each time the list changes, showing power, vectors and card swaps. Yama's lists and plan versions could share this.

## Not to copy
- All-caps labels ("VECTOR BREAKDOWN", "BEST", "TYPICAL") and middle-dot meta lines: against Yama's copy rules.
- The radar chart: a lot of space for five numbers that the bars below already show.
- Long per-card point breakdowns (+8, +5 ...) as the default view: Yama keeps methods behind one named disclosure.
- On a phone the art banner and three tiles push the list below the fold, and the title truncates.
- A sign-in nag bar on every tab.
