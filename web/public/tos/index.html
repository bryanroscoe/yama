Using Yama

  • Yama is free. There are no paid plans and nothing to buy.
  • Use it for playing and tracking games, decks and collections. Don't try to break into other accounts, overload the server, scrape it, or use it for anything unlawful.
  • Some features have fair-use limits (for example simulated games per day), so one account can't use up what everyone shares.
  • Be decent to the people you play with. Accounts that abuse Yama or other players may be turned off.
