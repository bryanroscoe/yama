Delete your Yama account

Yama, the Commander app by Bryan Roscoe, on the web at yama.ing and on Android.

In Yama

  1. Open Yama on the web or in the Android app, and sign in with the Google account you use for Yama.
  2. Go to Your profile, then Account settings, and choose Delete account.
  3. Type your account's email to confirm. The account is deleted at once.

Without signing in

Email bryanroscoe@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask for your account to be deleted. You get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account and everything stored with it: your name, email and Google account ID, decks and their versions, your collection and containers, deck plans and review answers, simulation runs, Ask Claude keys, settings and conversations, connected apps, script keys and sessions, and sharing links.

What stays, without your name

Other people's records stay whole. A game you played with other people stays in their history with your seat named "Former player"; a playgroup you run passes to another member; your place in someone else's playgroup becomes "Former member". None of these keep your name, email or account.

Deleted data leaves the live database at once. Copies of the database taken before server updates, kept to recover from a failed update, may still hold it until they are cleared.
