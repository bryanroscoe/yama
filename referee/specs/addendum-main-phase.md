# Spec: Addendum checks that the spell was cast during your main phase

Scenario: `card-unbreakable-formation` (generated by `referee/sweep.py`). Observed: Forge gives seat 0's Grizzly Bears a +1/+1 counter (3/3); phase leaves it 2/2 with no counter.

## 1. Card

- **Unbreakable Formation** {2}{W}, Instant. "Creatures you control gain indestructible until end of turn. Addendum — If you cast this spell during your main phase, put a +1/+1 counter on each of those creatures and they gain vigilance until end of turn."

Rulings (Scryfall):
- 2024-01-12: "Addendum abilities of instant spells apply while the spell is resolving, not immediately after casting it. If the spell is countered, you don't get the addendum bonus."
- 2024-01-12: "If an effect copies a spell with an addendum ability while it's on the stack, the copy wasn't cast at all, so you won't get the addendum bonus."
- 2019-01-25: it affects only creatures you control at the time it resolves.

## 2. Board and actions

Sweep board: seat 0 is the active player in its precombat main phase, with Plains, Wastes, Wastes and an untapped Grizzly Bears; Unbreakable Formation and a Wastes in hand. Seat 1 has Grizzly Bears, Serra Angel, Millstone, Seal of Fire. Seat 0 casts Unbreakable Formation (no targets) in its precombat main phase; seat 1 passes; it resolves.

## 3. Correct result

Seat 0's Grizzly Bears: indestructible and vigilance until end of turn, one +1/+1 counter, 3/3. Seat 1's creatures: unchanged (2/2 Bears, 4/4 Angel, no counters).

## 4. Why

1. "Addendum" is an ability word (207.2c): it has no rules meaning of its own; the sentence after it is read literally.
2. The condition is about when the spell was cast, and it was cast during seat 0's precombat main phase. The precombat main phase is a main phase (505.1: "The precombat and postcombat main phases are individually and collectively known as the main phase"). It was seat 0's own turn, so the condition is true.
3. On resolution (608.2c), "those creatures" are the creatures seat 0 controls at that time (ruling 2019-01-25): just its Grizzly Bears. Each gets a +1/+1 counter (122.1) and gains vigilance until end of turn, in addition to indestructible.

## 5. What phase did

The spell resolved, but no +1/+1 counter was placed (Bears stayed 2/2). Whether vigilance was granted isn't visible in the report.

## 6. Variations to test

- Same spell cast in seat 0's postcombat main phase: counter and vigilance (still "your main phase").
- Cast during combat (e.g. declare attackers step) or the end step of seat 0's turn: indestructible only, no counter, no vigilance.
- Cast during the opponent's main phase: no addendum ("your main phase").
- Cast in the main phase, then copied (e.g. by a copy effect): the copy gives no addendum bonus; the original does.
- Countered: nothing happens.
- Other addendum cards should follow the same condition, e.g. Sphinx's Insight ("Draw two cards. Addendum — If you cast this spell during your main phase, you gain 2 life.").

## 7. Acceptance checks

- Casting Unbreakable Formation in your own main phase with one creature leaves that creature with exactly one +1/+1 counter and vigilance and indestructible until end of turn.
- Casting it outside your main phase gives indestructible only.
- Opponents' creatures are never affected.
