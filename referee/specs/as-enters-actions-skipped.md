# Spec: "As this enters, [do something]" actions are skipped

Scenarios: `card-ixidron`, `card-living-lore` (generated by the card sweep, batch 2).

Both cards have a replacement effect that performs an action as the permanent enters. In phase the action didn't happen, so each creature's characteristic-defining power and toughness came out 0 and it died. Forge performed the action and its result is the correct one.

## 1. Cards

- **Ixidron** {3}{U}{U}, Creature — Illusion, */*. "As this creature enters, turn all other nontoken creatures face down. (They're 2/2 creatures.) / Ixidron's power and toughness are each equal to the number of face-down creatures on the battlefield."
  - Ruling 2006-09-25: "You turn the creatures face-down *as* Ixidron enters. There is never a moment when Ixidron is on the battlefield and the creatures are face-up."
  - Ruling 2006-09-25: "If Ixidron and another creature are entering at the same time, the other creature enters face up."
  - Ruling 2018-04-27: "Creatures turned face down by Ixidron are 2/2 creatures with no text, no name, no subtypes, no expansion symbol, and no mana cost."
  - Ruling 2006-09-25: "Turning a face-down creature face-down typically has no effect."
- **Living Lore** {3}{U}, Creature — Avatar, */*. "As this creature enters, exile an instant or sorcery card from your graveyard. / Living Lore's power and toughness are each equal to the exiled card's mana value. / Whenever this creature deals combat damage, you may sacrifice it. If you do, you may cast the exiled card without paying its mana cost."
  - Ruling 2018-12-07: "If there are no instant or sorcery cards in your graveyard as Living Lore enters the battlefield, it will enter the battlefield as a 0/0 creature and be put into its owner's graveyard due to having 0 toughness."
  - Ruling 2018-12-07: "If the exiled card somehow leaves exile while Living Lore is on the battlefield, it will become a 0/0 creature."

## 2. Board and actions

Board (the generated sweep board, `referee/sweep.py`): two players at 20 life, seat 0 active in its precombat main phase with an empty stack. Seat 0 controls exactly the lands that pay the card's mana cost (listed per card below) and an untapped Grizzly Bears (2/2 green Bear); its hand is the card and a Wastes; its graveyard holds Hill Giant (creature, mana value 4) and Divination (sorcery, mana value 3); its library is 8 Wastes. Seat 1 controls Grizzly Bears, Serra Angel (4/4 white Angel, flying, vigilance), Millstone (artifact) and Seal of Fire (enchantment); its hand is 2 Wastes; its graveyard holds Hill Giant; its library is 8 Wastes. Nothing is tapped and nothing has summoning sickness. Seat 0 casts the card with no optional or alternative costs. Targets given below were fixed for both engines.

- `card-ixidron`: seat 0's lands are Island, Island, Wastes, Wastes, Wastes. Seat 0 casts Ixidron; both players pass; it resolves.
- `card-living-lore`: seat 0's lands are Island, Wastes, Wastes, Wastes. Seat 0 casts Living Lore; it resolves.

## 3. Correct result

**Ixidron.** As Ixidron enters, seat 0's Grizzly Bears, seat 1's Grizzly Bears and seat 1's Serra Angel are turned face down. Each is a 2/2 creature with no name, no subtypes, no abilities and no mana cost, still untapped and still controlled by the same player. Ixidron is on the battlefield as a 3/3 Illusion. Nothing goes to a graveyard. (Forge's final state matches: three "(face down)" 2/2s and a 3/3 Ixidron.)

**Living Lore.** As Living Lore enters, seat 0 exiles Divination (the only instant or sorcery card in its graveyard). Living Lore is a 3/3 Avatar. Seat 0's graveyard is Hill Giant only; its exile is Divination. (Forge matches.)

## 4. Why

1. "As [this permanent] enters ..." is a replacement effect (614.1c) that modifies how the permanent enters (614.12). The action it describes is performed as part of the permanent entering, before it is on the battlefield; there is no window in which the permanent is on the battlefield and the action hasn't happened (Ixidron ruling).
2. Ixidron: turning the creatures face down gives them the face-down characteristics of 708.2a (2/2, no text, no name, no subtypes, no mana cost). Status face up/face down is a permanent's status, not a zone change (110.5), so they stay on the battlefield, untapped, under the same controllers. "Other nontoken creatures" excludes Ixidron itself and tokens (there are none here).
3. Ixidron's power and toughness are a characteristic-defining ability (604.3), applied in layer 7a. It counts face-down creatures on the battlefield: three. So Ixidron is 3/3 and survives state-based actions (704.5f only kills creatures with toughness 0 or less).
4. Living Lore: the exile happens as it enters (614.1c). Its P/T are defined by a characteristic-defining ability (604.3) that reads the exiled card's mana value: Divination's is 3. So Living Lore is 3/3.

## 5. What phase did

- Ixidron: entered, nothing was turned face down, Ixidron counted zero face-down creatures, was 0/0 and was put into the graveyard. All three Grizzly Bears/Serra Angel stayed face up.
- Living Lore: entered without exiling anything, was 0/0 and was put into the graveyard; Divination stayed in the graveyard.

(Other "as this enters, choose ..." cards in the same batch, such as Heraldic Banner and Morophon, did make a choice in phase, so the gap looks specific to as-enters actions that do something to other objects.)

## 6. Variations to test

- Ixidron with a creature token on the battlefield: the token stays face up and isn't counted unless something else made it face down.
- Ixidron with no other creatures: it enters as 0/0 and dies.
- Ixidron when a creature is already face down (e.g. a manifested or morphed creature): that creature is unaffected (708.2b) and still counted.
- Ixidron and another creature entering simultaneously: the other creature enters face up.
- After Ixidron has entered, a face-down creature leaves the battlefield: Ixidron's P/T drop by one at once.
- Living Lore with two instant/sorcery cards in the graveyard: its controller chooses which one; P/T equal that card's mana value.
- Living Lore with no instant or sorcery card in its owner's graveyard: 0/0, dies. It can't exile from an opponent's graveyard.
- Living Lore put onto the battlefield by an instant or sorcery spell: that spell is still on the stack and can't be exiled (ruling).

## 7. Acceptance checks

- After Ixidron resolves on this board, there are three face-down 2/2 creatures (one for seat 0, two for seat 1), all untapped, and Ixidron is a 3/3 on the battlefield. Both graveyards are unchanged.
- After Living Lore resolves on this board, Divination is in seat 0's exile, seat 0's graveyard is just Hill Giant, and Living Lore is a 3/3 on the battlefield.
